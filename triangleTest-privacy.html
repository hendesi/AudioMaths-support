Privacy Policy - Triangle Test

Last updated: 03.11.2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Triangle Test (“we”, “our”, “us”). Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Triangle Test handles your information when you use our iOS app (the “App”).

We are committed to transparency and privacy. Triangle Test does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All user data is stored locally on your device and is fully under your control.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Triangle Test does not collect or store:

  • Personal information (such as your name, email, or address)
  • Usage or analytics data
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Device identifiers (other than what Apple or RevenueCat may automatically handle)

All app-related data remains on your device and is removed automatically when you uninstall the App.

3. In-App Purchases and RevenueCat

Triangle Test may use RevenueCat to securely manage in-app purchases and subscriptions.

RevenueCat processes purchase-related information (such as your App Store receipt and subscription status) on our behalf to verify transactions and unlock premium features.

RevenueCat does not receive or store any personal information beyond what is necessary to process your purchase in compliance with Apple’s guidelines.

You can read RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy here: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4. Data Storage

All other data generated by the App (such as your preferences, scores, or progress) is stored locally on your device. We have no access to this data, and it is removed if you delete the App.

5. Children’s Privacy

Triangle Test is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or transmit any personal data from children or adults.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no personal data for you to access, modify, or delete. If you have questions about how your information is handled, you can contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available within the App or on our official website (if applicable). Any changes take effect immediately upon posting.
